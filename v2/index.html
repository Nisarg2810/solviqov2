06 The honest questions

The things people actually ask on the first call.

"Two weeks sounds too fast. What is the catch?"

The catch is the scope. We build one workflow, not a department. Anything needing five user roles and three approval chains is two sprints, and we say so on the call rather than after you sign.

"Our ERP partner says only they should touch Fusion."

They are right, and we do not touch it. The app sits beside Fusion and talks to it through its own API. Your instance, your configuration, unchanged.

"Will this end up as another system nobody updates?"

That happens when a tool adds a step. This one removes a step: the inspection is the record, so there is no second place to type it. If a workflow cannot lose a step, we will tell you it is the wrong thing to build.

"What happens after launch?"

You get the documentation and you keep our number. Changes are one plain sentence, not a change request form.
