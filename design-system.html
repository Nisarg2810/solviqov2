Apply depth to both
  • NO LIGHTEvery border is the same 1px grey on all four sides. Real objects catch light on top and fall into shadow underneath. Fix: one light source, top left, forever.
  • NO RAMPColours are single values. Designers work in ramps of 6 to 8 steps so tints and shades are already decided. Fix: four full ramps.
  • NO HEIGHTCards, page and header all sit on the same plane. Fix: a four step elevation ladder with ambient and direct shadow.
  • NO MATERIALPerfectly smooth dark surfaces read as plastic. Fix: film grain, sheen, brushed chrome, frosted glass.
  • ONE ACCENTAmber alone cannot carry both "act now" and "this is data". Fix: a cool second accent in tension with it.
  • DEAD MOTIONThings fade in once and then sit still forever. Fix: continuous life, parallax, and surfaces that answer the cursor.

09 Rules

What holds it together.

Always
  • Light from the top left. Inner highlight on top, shadow below.
  • Pick from the ramp. Never invent a one off colour.
  • Grain on every dark surface, at 30 to 35 percent.
  • Amber and signal together stay under a tenth of the screen.
  • Every section owns one quiet living element.
  • Motion answers the cursor. Cards tilt, buttons shine.
  • Every heading over 40px takes the chrome gradient.
Never
  • A flat 1px border on all four sides of a raised object.
  • Two accent colours competing in the same block.
  • Stock photography, anywhere, for any reason.
  • Pure black or pure white as a surface.
  • A gradient on body text, or on anything under 40px.
  • Animation that loops in the corner of the eye with no purpose.
  • More than one marquee on a page.
