03 The gap

Most businesses outgrow their software long before they can justify a big IT project.

Off the shelf tools cover the first eighty percent. The rest, the part that makes your business yours, ends up in spreadsheets, group chats and someone's memory.

01

The long queue

Big agencies and IT teams price work around big projects. A tool you need this month waits behind contracts worth far more, for months, and nobody is to blame.

The disconnected tools
