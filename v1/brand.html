The Sweep mark, standardized.

This version replaces the earlier segment-S identity. The Sweep mark, three chevrons rising along a gauge arc, is now the system of record for the logo, the color palette and every application shown below. Everything here is what actually ships on the live site.

01

Rationale

Why this mark, and why chrome and amber instead of the ink and fog system it replaces.

Why the Sweep mark

Three chevrons, increasing in size and brightness, bent onto the arc a gauge needle actually travels. It reads as speed and as instrumentation at once, the two things a company promising four week delivery to a fleet and HSSE audience needs to say without saying them.

Why chrome and amber

Brushed chrome on near black reads as precision hardware, not consumer software. Amber is the one warm signal in the system, reserved for calls to action and live status, echoing the safety amber already on every hard hat and hazard sign our clients work around daily.

02

Mark System

The Sweep mark is built from one repeating unit chevron, scaled, rotated and positioned along a fixed arc. No freehand curves.

UNIT CHEVRONOne shape, points "-27,-30 28,0 -27,30", reused at three scales: 0.62, 0.82 and 1.05.
ARC PATHCenters sit on the quadratic curve M70,168 Q120,95 172,72, rotated -25°, -12° and 2° to follow the arc's own tangent.
BRIGHTNESS RAMPSteel dim to steel mid to a brushed chrome gradient. Each step in size is also a step in contrast.
SQUARE CAPS ONLYstroke-linecap: square on every segment. Rounded caps are not an approved variant.

08

Governance

The short version of everything above.

Always
  • Use the exact unit chevron and arc coordinates, never redrawn by eye
  • Keep the brightness ramp, dim to mid to chrome, in that order
  • Reserve amber for buttons and live status only
Never
  • Recolor, rotate or round the chevrons
  • Set the wordmark in anything but Rajdhani
  • Use green or red decoratively, they are status colors only
