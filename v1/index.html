01
The gap

Somewhere between your ERP and your ops team, the work falls through.

Oracle Fusion can do almost anything, eventually. Your HSSE manager needs a violation logged today. So the real operation runs on spreadsheets, WhatsApp threads and someone's memory, while the system of record collects dust.

01

The six month queue

Your Oracle Fusion partner has a backlog priced around module rollouts. A one screen change request sits behind bigger contracts for months, not because anyone is careless, because that is how the incentives are built.

02

The spreadsheet operation

Violations, inspections, room assignments, all tracked in files that live on one laptop and break the moment two people edit them at once. Nobody trusts the numbers, so everybody double checks them by hand.

03

The invisible approval

Sign off happens over email and WhatsApp. Nobody can say, with certainty and a timestamp, what was approved, by whom, and under what version of the request.
